AI-Based Chatbot for Oral Health Education is a proposed AI-powered web platform designed to provide accessible, reliable, and personalized oral health education. It offers two separate chatbot experiences for learners and patients using Artificial Intelligence and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG). The system provides dental learning resources, oral-care guidance, Tamil and English support, voice interaction, quizzes, and personalized reminders. Its main aim is to improve oral health awareness and promote preventive dental care through trusted information.
