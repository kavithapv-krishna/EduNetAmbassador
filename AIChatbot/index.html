ABOUT BRIGHTBITE

Helpful information.
Honest boundaries.

BrightBite is a small educational prototype designed to make common oral-health topics feel easier to explore. Its chat responses are prewritten examples, not generated advice.

Everyone’s needs are different. For questions about your own teeth, gums, symptoms, or care, speak with a qualified dental professional.
